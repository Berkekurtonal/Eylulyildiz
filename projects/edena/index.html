EDENA

Paradise Hidden in the Heart of the City

Type
Hotel · Urban Oasis
Location
İçerenköy, Ataşehir, Istanbul
02

Overview

A hotel positioned as an oasis of calm that reunites nature and humanity, offering not only a comfortable stay but an experience that soothes the soul.

Ivy hanging from the walls, indoor gardens and a courtyard full of birdsong are designed to help guests reconnect with nature. The name comes from Eden, a symbol of peace and abundance in ancient texts.
